doc.player

This page is not mindX. It is a bare HTML file that mounts doc.player against a backend and gets the whole voaicey surface: transport, timeline, volume and balance, oscilloscope and spectrum with zoom, the speaker cast, and the dreamknob deck.

Point base at anything that answers the two-shape contract in src/doc-player.js. Against a live mindX it is /listen/<name>; against a mock it is whatever you serve.

Press LISTEN. The panel opens on the right and starts playing in the same gesture, because an open panel with a silent PLAY button asks you to press a second button to do the thing you already asked for.
